3 · Principles & explorations

What the landscape suggests

Interpretation and early sketches. Not specs.

Understand

  • Keep provenanceEvery summary line links to its comments.
  • Keep frequencyCount people per theme.
  • Show coverage"Read 14 of 16 files."
  • Group by action"Needs owner" beats topic.

Act

  • Filter, then actShow the batch before applying.
  • Keep attributionCredit every commenter.
  • Proposed ≠ decidedAI suggestions look different.
  • Comments as dataAPI, CLI or MCP for agents.

Learn

  • Choosable scopeEvery rule says how far it reaches.
  • Show evidenceWhich comments, how many people.
  • Test before applyPreview on past questions.
  • LifecycleCandidate → promoted → retired.
Framework

One loop from feedback to context

Understand captures and clusters. Act decides. Learn applies, verifies and retires. Most data products stop at Apply.

4 · Open questions

Decisions ahead

Strategy

Which pillar is our product's job, and in what order?

Understand is table stakes; Act and Learn still differentiate.

Users

Whose comments: human, human-to-agent, or agent?

The largest gap is human comments feeding Learn.

Learn

Who can promote a comment to a workspace rule?

Without an owner per scope, rules conflict.

Act

What is the unit of a batch?

For data, "this metric" may beat "this folder".

Success

What proves it worked?

Repeat comments, benchmark accuracy, analyst escalations.

Trust

What must never be automated?

Data tools keep a human approval step; Bugbot auto-promotes.
